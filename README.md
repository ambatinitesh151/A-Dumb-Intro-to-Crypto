# A-Dumb-Intro-to-Crypto

*A Roblox data-center tycoon and fictional cryptocurrency market simulator.*

## About the Project

**A-Dumb-Intro-to-Crypto** is my Congressional App Challenge project. It is a Roblox tycoon game where players build and manage data centers to mine fictional cryptocurrencies, expand their operations, research better equipment, and participate in a dynamic in-game market.

Players must manage resources such as cooling, energy, and internet connectivity to keep their servers operating. As their operations grow, they can unlock better equipment, increase their production capabilities, and sell their fictional coins as market prices fluctuate.

The project combines tycoon gameplay, resource management, research progression, and economic simulation.

## Why I Made This

I wanted to explore cryptocurrency through an interactive game that could introduce players to some of its risks, including market volatility, sudden price crashes, and rug pulls.

While cryptocurrency can create opportunities, it also involves significant risks and uncertainty. I wanted to simplify some of these concepts into a cartoonish, interactive tycoon game that demonstrates how unpredictable markets can affect decisions and lead to losses.

All cryptocurrencies and market activity in the game are fictional. The game is intended for educational and entertainment purposes, not as financial advice or a recommendation to invest in real cryptocurrency.

## Features

- **Custom building and placement system:** Build and expand a data-center facility.
- **Server management:** Operate servers with cooling, energy, and internet requirements.
- **Fictional cryptocurrencies:** Produce and sell in-game coins.
- **Dynamic markets:** Coin prices fluctuate over time.
- **Independent server markets:** Each Roblox server has its own evolving market state.
- **Research progression:** Unlock better servers and equipment through a research tree.
- **Multiple currencies:** Manage Cash, KeyPoints, and RP as part of progression.
- **Tycoon gameplay:** Expand infrastructure and improve your operations over time.
